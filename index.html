About Xfiniti Technologies

We build AI Agents that work
so your team doesn't have to.

Xfiniti Technologies is an AI product development company designing and deploying intelligent AI Agents for businesses of all sizes — from global enterprises to local retailers. We help organisations automate their most time-consuming processes, integrate AI into their existing workflows, and build proprietary agents that deliver measurable outcomes.

Whether you need an AI Agent that handles customer enquiries around the clock, automates your back-office operations, or monitors and responds to cybersecurity threats autonomously — we build, train, and deploy it end-to-end.

Our international team spans Sydney, India, and San Francisco, giving us the reach to support clients across Asia-Pacific, North America, and beyond. We combine deep technical engineering with genuine product thinking — every agent we build is designed to be practical, reliable, and immediately impactful.

Alongside our AI Agent practice, Xfiniti runs an enterprise cybersecurity division and a consumer apps portfolio — making us one of a rare group of technology companies operating successfully across all three disciplines simultaneously.
